Privacy & terms

Applies to this website and the Cuprea web game it hosts. Last updated 2026-10-06.

The short version

The game runs entirely in your browser. It has no accounts, no chat, no trackers and no analytics of its own. Your factory is saved on your device. Nothing leaves your device unless you press a button that says so.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your worlds (one per profile), settings, saved blueprints, Daily Factory results and any bug-report notes you write, in your browser's local storage. They stay on this device and in this browser.
  • Clearing site data, using a private window, or some browsers after a long time without a visit (Safari) can remove them. Settings → Export saves a backup file; Import restores it.

What we receive from you

  • Nothing automatically. The game does not phone home.
  • Play stats, only if you press "Send play stats" (in Stats). That sends an anonymous summary: levels completed, time played, machine counts, settings, screen size, browser name and game version. It contains no name, email, account, advertising ID or device ID, and the server does not store IP addresses or user agents. It is used to see where players get stuck and to decide what to build next.
  • Bug reports are written into a text box for you to copy or save. Only what you choose to send to us, by whatever channel is listed in the game, reaches us. Please do not include personal information in them.

Advertising on this website

This version of the website shows no advertisements. The rest of this section describes what would apply if one is ever added. On wide screens the website would show one advertisement beside the game, served by Google AdSense. We request non-personalised ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and Google may process your IP address and browser information. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. Where the law requires it, a consent message is shown before any ad is loaded.

The game page on itch.io and the downloadable version show no ads.

Children

The game is for a general audience and asks for no personal information from anyone, of any age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example inside a bug report), contact us and we will delete it.
