What it is

Cuprea is a factory-building game without combat. Dig up wood, stone and ore, run it along belts into furnaces and assemblers, and deliver what the Hub asks for. Each level unlocks a new machine, and the puzzle is always the same gentle one: where does the bottleneck go next?

Programmable delivery botsGive a bot a short list of steps and it will ferry cargo across your factory - no belt required.
Every level unlocks somethingSplitters, tunnels, filters, depots, fabricators, trading posts and more. Freeplay goals after the campaign.
Share what you buildCopy any part of your factory as a text code, paste it into a friend's game, or take a Photo of the whole thing.
Stop whenever you likeNothing decays while you are away and there are no timers. Every level end is a good place to stop.

How to play (two minutes)

  • Press 2 for the Extractor and drop it on a coloured deposit. Its arrow shows where the items come out.
  • Press 1 for Belts and drag a line from the extractor into the Hub in the middle. R rotates.
  • Watch the level panel fill up. When it completes, pick the next goal or stop - the game saved already.
  • On a phone or tablet: tap Build, pick a tool, drag to place. Pinch to zoom.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. This web version is the full game as it exists today. If it ever comes to Steam, that version will be a paid download with extra content, and the web version stays free.

Where is my save?

In this browser, on this device (browser storage). Clearing site data deletes it, and private windows forget it. Use Settings → Export (or Copy) to back it up, and Import to bring it back on another device.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, with touch controls. A desktop with a mouse is the best way to play; big factories are easier with a keyboard.

Are there ads?

Not in this version. If one small ad ever appears beside the game on wide screens, it will be to help pay for hosting; it would never interrupt play, and the privacy page says what the ad provider could collect.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Open Settings → Bug report & comments inside the game. It writes a report you can copy or save, with optional anonymous play stats, and tells you where to send it.
